# Queenie — Murder Drones / Meta Runner bases

Original Encore fan-game crossovers. Six appearances, two individual native PNGs per appearance, no animation.

Identity anchor inspected: official GLITCH Queenie Animatez full figure, https://glitchproductions.store/products/queenie-animatez (native image recorded in manifest), then official plush https://glitchproductions.store/products/queenie-plush as secondary. Brown chess queen crown and finial, half-lidded white eyes with warm orange/red-brown irises, lower eyelashes, detached white gloved hands and robe-covered chess base. No human hair, mouth, legs or feet. The exaggerated unequal eye placement of the plush is not used as anatomical authority.

Universe sources: https://www.glitchprod.com/murder-drones and https://www.glitchprod.com/meta-runner. Their imagery establishes universe mood, not a canonical Queenie crossover.

Existing art: assets/encore/selection/queenie.png at PR head 616a585274506f20c54386d8be07a76e75e1f198 (review copy supplied by root). New designs use a clean high-detail 2D painted/cel-shaded style with readable contours and native alpha. The inspected Animatez figure establishes the face proportions; plush distortions are not copied.
